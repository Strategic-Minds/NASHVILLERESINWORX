# NRW Content Ops Approval Matrix

## Autonomous Without Approval

- Inspect approved Drive folders.
- Create draft content ideas.
- Score opportunities.
- Generate hooks, scripts, captions, blog snippets, and internal reports.
- Create dry-run publishing queue rows.
- Produce daily and weekly digests.
- Analyze verified analytics exports.

## Approval Required

- Public publishing.
- Paid ads.
- Pricing, warranty, training, certification, safety, or performance claims.
- Customer-identifying assets or testimonials.
- Bulk email/SMS.
- Live production deploys.
- Supabase migrations.
- Domain, env var, billing, or platform credential changes.
- High-risk social replies.

## Blocked Unless Explicitly Enabled

- Auto-spend ad budget.
- Auto-publish to unverified accounts.
- Use copyrighted third-party assets without rights.
- Expose customer personal data.
- Change infrastructure or database policies without review.

## Risk Levels

- `low`: generic educational content, no customer identity, no unsupported claims.
- `medium`: offer/CTA content, project examples, before/after language, local SEO claims.
- `high`: pricing, warranty, safety, customer names/faces, paid promotions, regulated claims.
